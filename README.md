# HR Attrition Analysis- Power BI
HR Employee Attrition Analysis using Power BI with key business insights.

## Project Overview

An HR analytics project using SQL and Power BI to analyze employee attrition patterns and identify employee groups with higher attrition.
The analysis covers 1,470 employee records and focuses on factors such as department, job role, age, salary, overtime, job satisfaction, and work-life balance.

## Objective

- Identify departments and job roles with higher employee attrition
- Analyze salary and demographic patterns related to attrition
- Understand the relationship between job satisfaction and work-life balance
- Identify high-risk employee segments
- Provide data-driven insights to support employee retention strategies

##  Tools & Technologies
1. SQL / MySQL – Data analysis and business queries
2. Microsoft Power BI – Interactive dashboard
3. DAX – KPI measures and calculations
4. Excel / CSV – Dataset

## Business Requirement

1. What is the overall employee attrition rate?
2. Which departments have the highest attrition?
3. Which job roles show higher attrition?
4. Does salary level affect employee attrition?
5. Which age groups have higher attrition?
6. Does work-life balance relate to employee attrition?
7.Which employee segments may require greater retention focus?

## Key Findings

1. Overall employee attrition rate is 16.12%.
2. Sales has the highest department attrition at 20.63%.
3. Sales Representatives show the highest displayed job-role attrition at 39.76%.
4. Low Salary Band has the highest attrition at 28.61%.
5. Employees with Poor Work-Life Balance show the highest attrition at 31.25%.
6. Under-25 employees have the highest age-group attrition at approximately 39%.
7. The analysis helps HR identify high-risk employee segments and focus retention strategies.
